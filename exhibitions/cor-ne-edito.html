Cor ne edito

June 22, 2023Cultural Center Casa Melchor Pinto, Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia

"Do not consume the heart" is the phrase that inspires this art exhibition, where we explore various nuances of human anguish through the unique expression of twelve watercolor portraits of women. These artworks become intimate portraits that reveal the subtleties and complexities of this emotion, inviting the viewer to immerse themselves in its depth, capturing the fragility and ephemeral beauty that lie within it.

The portrayed characters convey a melancholic expression that evokes a range of subtle emotions, from nostalgia to contemplative serenity. The melancholic expressions on the faces of the depicted women reflect a sense of vulnerability and profound contemplation, delving into a dark realm within the human psyche.

Through each brushstroke and detail, we seek to convey the complexity of anguish, where emotion intertwines with sadness, introspection, and the stillness of the soul. Through visual representation, we aim to establish an intimate connection between the viewer and the protagonists of these artworks, allowing melancholy to serve as a bridge to personal reflection and shared empathy. Each portrait is a window into a unique inner world, reminding us that sadness and beauty often intertwine in the human experience.

This exhibition is a tribute to the depth of emotion and a reminder that even in moments of vulnerability, we find the beauty and connection that shape our shared humanity. Through these watercolors, we delve into the richness of melancholic expression and open ourselves to a deeper understanding of ourselves and others.
